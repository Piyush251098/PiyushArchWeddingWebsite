Our Story

How it all began

It started with a train to Portugal.

We were both studying in Germany, living our own lives and travelling with our friends. Then, at a train station on the way to Portugal, our paths happened to cross. We didn't know it then, but that little moment would become the beginning of our story.

Somewhere between the conversations, the gym and good food

We found ourselves talking a little more, laughing a little more, and looking forward to seeing each other. And then there was food — especially the kind worth talking about.

And, of course, pizza.

He had a habit of showing up with pizza. But somewhere along the way, it wasn't really about the pizza. It was the way he remembered the little things, helped without being asked, and always found a way to take care of her.

The little things

It was the little things.

  • A chance meeting.
  • A shared love for food.
  • A few workouts.
  • A lot of conversations.
  • Countless small gestures.

And somehow, all those little things became something much bigger.
